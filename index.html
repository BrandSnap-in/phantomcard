Support & FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

SE, Air, and Pro batteries are replaced by a Phantom service centre in a single visit. Ultra's solar cell is designed to outlast the product and typically never needs replacement.

A thin shielding layer inside the card blocks the radio frequency used by contactless skimmers, so your cards can't be read without physical contact with your own device.

Guardian AI processes signals like location, motion, and time on-device wherever possible. It's built to infer risk, not to record or share a log of your movements.

Every Phantom Card ships with a 1-year hardware warranty covering manufacturing defects, extendable through Phantom+ plans.

Cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period, and your hardware keeps working without a subscription.
